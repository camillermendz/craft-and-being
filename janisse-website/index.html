Option 01

Strategy Consultation

Clarity, without an ongoing engagement

$299 USD

This is the right fit if you want expert direction before continuing the website independently. We’ll use one focused hour to look at everything together and make decisions in real time.

What’s included

  • One 60-minute video consultation
  • Live review of the website, copy, messaging, design, and user experience
  • Strategic recommendations and clear next steps provided on the call
  • Space to bring your current build, questions, assets, and ideas to the table

This option is designed as an on-the-spot consultation and does not include ongoing back-and-forth, design revisions, development, or file delivery.

Option 02

Refinement & Migration

A strategic polish for the site you create

$650 USD

This is the best path if you’re comfortable completing the core website in Hostinger and want me to step in for the final refinement, technical handoff, and launch.

What’s included

  • A strategic review of the completed build and exported files
  • Refinement of visual details, responsiveness, and functionality
  • A Loom walkthrough with my revisions and strategic perspective
  • Two revision rounds following the initial review
  • Transfer into a GitHub repository and deployment through Vercel
  • Full domain connection and transfer support

You’ll provide clean exported HTML, CSS, and JavaScript files and cover the Hostinger plan required to export them. Figma and Framer recreation are not included. If the exported files require substantial rebuilding, additional work is billed at $135 USD/hour and approved with you before I proceed.

Option 03

Full Website Design & Build

The most cohesive, hands-off path

$1,200 USD

This is the strongest fit if you want the strategy, design, and build shaped as one cohesive experience from the beginning, using the thoughtful groundwork you’ve already gathered.

What’s included

  • Three custom pages: Home, Speaking, and My Story
  • Design and development using your copy, brand, photography, and existing assets

  • Initial concept presentation and approval
  • Two revision rounds after initial concept approval
  • Framer build, organized GitHub handoff, Vercel deployment, and domain connection

The investment begins at $900 USD for a one-page website. Each additional page is $150 USD, bringing the proposed three-page scope to $1,200 USD. Any third-party hosting, domain, platform, or subscription costs are paid separately by you.
