The Scope of Support

Full-Service Social Media Management

All the regular rhythms of managing your channels: content ideation and creative direction, filming and curating footage, caption writing, graphic design, scheduling and publishing, and reporting on reach and engagement. Instagram and TikTok are the focus, with Facebook linked to Instagram and Pinterest kept as a passive auto-sync.

Email-Led Content Planning

The Sunday Series and liturgical living emails serve as the spine of the calendar. Each week's subject matter maps into Instagram and TikTok content: reels, voiceovers, and lifestyle footage drawn from the existing Dropbox and phone library, including the Kansas City printer trip footage.

Writers, Voices & Influencers

Sunday Series writers are invited into content at their own comfort level, whether voiceover, full reel, or photos only, with no one required to show their face. The influencer program starts small and intentional: warm existing relationships first, micro and nano creators scouted on TikTok, a standard offer and rate sheet, and close tracking of each post's reach and engagement.

Seasonal Launch Support

Launch moments are folded into the ongoing rhythm as they come. This fall, that includes the 10th edition Mass Journal arriving October 7th alongside the new totes, pouch, and blank journal, with messaging that sets expectations for the edition's refinements and carries this year's red-and-green trees theme through First Look on November 9th.

Workflow & Rhythm

A Notion dashboard houses link drops, content approval, platform views, reuse mapping, and influencer coordination, replacing the current spreadsheet setup. The engagement begins with one strategy session, followed by a set weekly review-and-approval day and regular check-ins to assess performance.

The Investment

Camille and Kate begin with an audit, inventory of existing drive and content, and shaping a content cadence. Together with Kassie and Christie, we clarify the vision for ESS content marketing; from there, Camille and Kate carry out the strategy as one coordinated creative team. Pricing is a flat monthly rate for the duration of the engagement to keep the partnership clear and easy to plan around.
